Academic Integrity Academic integrity is a fundamental pillar in educational environments  It involves preventing plagiarism, cheating, academic falsification  unfair academic advantages, aiding dishonesty unauthorized access to official academic documents, and intellectual property violations 
Mechanisms for Ensuring Academic Integrity The IT Department promotes academic integrity among students and faculty The university provides tools to faculty to detect violations of academic integrity Students must submit original work for all assignments exams and research 
Forms of Academic Integrity Violations Cheating: Unauthorized use of materials during exams or assignments  Plagiarism: Using others ' work without proper attribution  Exploiting others ' efforts : Having others complete assignments on their behalf Impersonation: Claiming to be another student in academic settings Forgery: Altering or misleading information in academic work
Mechanisms for Detecting Violations The university uses " SafeAssign " to check for plagiarism generating detailed reports that compare student work against online sources and databases 
Disciplinary Procedures for Identity Fraud and Cheating A permanent, centralized disciplinary committee at the college level is responsible for handling student violations  After verifying a violation the committee may impose one or more of the following penalties: Verbal warnings documented with a written commitment or formal written warnings  Temporary suspension from student activities or certain university services  Denial of academic recommendations  Cancellation of exam results in cases of cheating or attempts  Suspension from studies for at least one semester and up to two semesters, with suspension of any scholarships if applicable Permanent expulsion from the university
In Case of a Disciplinary Decision: Disciplinary actions are documented in the student s file. Permanent expulsion results in the student being unable to re-enroll in the university The disciplinary decision may be announced within the university The student s guardian may be notified of the decision
